# Credits

Every asset that ships with the game is listed here with its source, author and licence. Map and terrain data attributions are in the README under "Data and credits".
Only CC0 sources (Poly Haven, Kenney, Quaternius, ambientCG) or CC-BY assets credited here are allowed.

## Props (`apps/game/public/models/props/*.glb`)

All prop models are original work: they are generated by the deterministic Blender scripts in
`tools/props/` (run `sh tools/props/build.sh`) from primitives, with vertex colours taken from
`world/style/palette.json` plus a few neutral steel, foliage and rust tones. No third-party meshes,
textures or photographs are used, so there is nothing to attribute. The models are covered by the repository's MIT licence.

## Characters (`apps/game/public/models/characters/*.glb`)

Bodies and animation clips come from two Quaternius packs released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public domain dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not required; the author is listed here anyway. Both packs share one 65-bone humanoid skeleton, so the clips bind to the bodies by bone name. Everything else (clothes, skin tones, topi, skirts and tunics) is original work built by `tools/characters/build.py` (`sh tools/characters/fetch.sh <dir>` downloads the two packs; see `tools/characters/README.md`), covered by the repository's MIT licence.
