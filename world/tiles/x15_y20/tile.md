# x15_y20 — सामाखुसी (Samakhusi)

- The tile covers Samakhusi in north Kathmandu, a residential and school district that sits between Gongabu to the north, Ranibari to the east, Balaju to the west and Golkopakha towards Thamel to the south. Samakhusi Marg has long been a commuting road for people travelling from Tokha and Nuwakot into the city centre.
- About 2,600 footprints are mapped, mostly multi-storey RCC houses packed along lanes of 4–7 m; the Town Planning blocks in the north follow a regular street pattern.
- Samakhusi Marg (7 m, mapped as secondary) runs north to south through the western half of the tile and carries the shops, a clinic, a mart and fast-food counters, so it is the street with the signs and awnings. Taxis start here.
- Schools, colleges and pre-schools are scattered through the lanes (Greenland School and College, Navodit College, Mother International Academy, Euro Kids Pre School), so the tile is busy on foot at school hours.
- Two small parks, Samakhusi Park and the Town Planning park, break up the blocks, and the British Cemetery touches the southern edge.
- Low parts of the area flood in heavy monsoon rain.
- Sensory detail: school bells, shutters rattling up on Samakhusi Marg, and two-wheelers cutting through the lanes.

Data notes: six footprints under 8 m² are removed as slivers. Default storeys are raised to four to match the RCC infill. The church is tagged as a place of worship, which the style picker would draw as a brick temple, so it is styled by id as a gabled institutional building. Footprints tagged as colleges and kindergartens are also styled by id.
