# x17_y16 — घट्टेकुलो (Ghattekulo)

- Ghattekulo is a residential neighbourhood in central-east Kathmandu, near Maitidevi and Devi Nagar and a little under a kilometre east of Singha Durbar, on the flat valley floor at about 1,300 m.
- The tile is almost entirely dense reinforced-concrete housing, mostly four to six storeys, packed along narrow lanes.
- Rudramati Marg is the main through road: it enters from the north-east corner, bends west, then runs south across the middle of the tile. Tanka Prasad Marg runs along the west side.
- Ground floors along the lanes hold small offices and businesses: travel agents, print shops, IT firms and training centres are all mapped here.
- Schools, a ward office and a few small Hindu temples sit among the houses, so the lanes are busy with students and walkers on school days.
- Sensory detail: scooter horns in the lanes, shop shutters rolling up, and the smell of incense near the lane-corner shrines.

Data notes: the default storey count is raised to four because the surrounding blocks are visibly taller than the suburb default. Two small footprints whose centres lie on a road centreline are removed. The Mandala Theatre footprint is still mapped here, although the company has since moved to Thapagaun.
