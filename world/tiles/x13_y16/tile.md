# x13_y16 — कालिमाटी (Kalimati)

- Kalimati lies beside the Bishnumati river, south-west of the old city, and is best known for the Kalimati fruit and vegetable market, the largest wholesale produce market in Nepal. It opened in 1987 as a government wholesale centre and still supplies the valley daily.
- The district is dense, unplanned RCC: four-to-five-storey mixed-use blocks with shops at street level, packed along lanes a few metres wide.
- Ganeshman Singh Path (गणेशमान सिंह पथ) is the main trunk road through the tile, four lanes carrying the market traffic. Tankeshwar Road (टंकेश्वर सडक) and Bishnumati Track Road run north through the east of the tile and cross the river on the Bishnumati bridges (बिष्णुमती पुल).
- The west of the tile holds the engineering-college campus blocks (Block C, Block E, Old Drawing Hall, Canteen) and two hospitals (Chest Hospital, Shree Jyoti Hospital); the south-east holds a compound of long, single-storey government buildings.
- Two Hindu shrines, Nil Barahi Temple and Tankeshwar Mahadev Temple, stand in the north-east, a short walk from the market.
- Sensory detail: early-morning truck horns, shouted auction prices and the smell of cut greens and fish around the market gates.
