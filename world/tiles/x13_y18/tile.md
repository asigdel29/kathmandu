# x13_y18 — चमती (Chamati)

- The tile is a dense residential district on the west bank of the Bishnumati river, less than a kilometre east of Swayambhu hill. The mapped road beside the river is named Chamati Bishnumati Kinar (चमती बिष्णुमती किनार).
- Swayambhu Marg (स्वयम्भू मार्ग) is the main road: a secondary road that crosses the tile from east to west toward the hill, with Pushpalal Path (पुष्पलाल पथ) running down the east side.
- Almost all of the 2,800 mapped buildings are RCC houses of three to five storeys packed along narrow lanes; about three dozen are schools, and a few residential blocks are tagged five or six storeys.
- Bijeshwori Temple (Vidheshvari / Bijeshwori Bahal) stands on the west bank of the Bishnumati in the Dhalko area, a small courtyard temple closed in by the houses around it.
- Shobha Bhagwati, a Hindu goddess shrine on the river bank at the north of the tile, is a focus of the Dashain festival.
- Sensory detail: a brick temple courtyard a few metres across, hemmed in by concrete walls and rooftop water tanks, with microbuses calling at the two bus stops on the east edge.

Data notes: the Bijeshwori and Ganesh footprints carry no height in OSM, so they are given storey counts; Sanghaaram Bihar has no worship tag on its footprint, so it is styled as a temple by override. No footprint crosses a road in this tile, so nothing is removed. Default storeys are raised from 3 to 4.
