# x14_y20 — नयाँ बजार (Naya Bazar)

- Naya Bazar sits in Kathmandu ward 16, the north-west ward that also holds Thamel and the Balaju industrial estate; the Ring Road runs through the middle of the ward, and in this tile it crosses the west side as the Kathmandu Ringroad trunk with Lekhnath Sadak joining it at Balaju Chowk.
- The Bishnumati river cuts the tile diagonally from the north-east to the south-west corner, with the Bishnumati kinar roads (बिष्णुमती किनार) running down each bank.
- Almost all of the 2,598 footprints are small RCC houses and shops packed into lanes off Mhepi Marg, Parijat Sadak and Manoram Marg; the tile default is raised to four storeys to match that density.
- Schools and hospitals take up a large share of the larger footprints: OSM tags 29 school buildings and 4 hospital buildings here, and Silverline Hospital, a non-profit hospital on Lekhnath Sadak at Balaju Chowk, has its lot just north-west of the Ring Road junction.
- Gurudwara Nanak Math (tagged in OSM as Gurunayak Math), a Sikh site on the Bishnumati bank on the road to Balaju, stands on a wooded rise in the south of the tile, higher than the river road below it.

Data notes: the zone stays `suburb`. School, college and hospital footprints are restyled `institutional` with their tagged levels (three or four when untagged); the party palace and Ncell Centre are `commercial_glass`; the petrol pump is `tin_shed`; the two small shrine footprints on the west edge are `temple_brick`. The large courtyard block at (372, 23) has no tags and is set to two storeys so it does not rise to the tile default. No footprint touches a road centreline or the river polygon, so nothing is removed.
