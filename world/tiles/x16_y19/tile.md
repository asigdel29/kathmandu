# x16_y19 — खरेल टोल (Kharel Tole, Lazimpat–Gairidhara)

- The tile sits in the Lazimpat–Gairidhara area north-east of Thamel and the Narayanhiti palace grounds. Lazimpat is known for its hotels, restaurants, schools, department stores and embassies, and the primary road (Lazimpat Sadak, becoming Narayan Gopal Sadak) cuts across the north-west of the tile.
- West of that road the buildings are hotels, banks and travel and trekking offices; the OSM data has Radisson Hotel Kathmandu on the west edge, and Hotel Ambassador, Hotel Tibet and Hotel Hiranya nearby.
- The east and middle of the tile is a grid of three- to four-storey RCC houses on narrow lanes (Nil Saraswati Marga, Shree Marga, Shakuna Marga), with a lot of schools, colleges and kindergartens mixed in.
- Gairidhara Sadak runs east–west along the south edge from the Nag Pokhari junction, and a small "Tarkari bazar" (vegetable market) is mapped at its north side near (595, 772).
- Small shrines are dotted through the lanes: Nil Saraswati and a Saraswati mandir stand a few metres apart at (607–617, 470–475), and a small Sweta Kali Mai temple stands at the east edge.

Data notes: the zone stays `suburb` with the default raised to four storeys to match the visible density. No footprint crosses a road centreline, so nothing is removed. Awnings and signs follow Lazimpat Sadak and Gairidhara Sadak; shrines stand at the two Saraswati POIs that have no footprint.
