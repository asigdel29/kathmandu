# x17_y14 — बुद्धनगर (Buddhanagar)

- Buddhanagar is a built-up residential neighbourhood in south-east Kathmandu, next to Minbhawan, Sankhamul and Baneshwor, beside the Bagmati river, which marks the boundary with Lalitpur here.
- Sankhamul, next door, is one of the valley's sacred river confluences, so the shrines scattered through the lanes here belong to a place that has been visited for ritual bathing and cremation for centuries.
- The tile is dense reinforced-concrete housing, mostly four storeys, with ground-floor shops, hostels and small offices along the lanes and larger stores and banks in the north-east corner.
- It holds an unusual number of schools and colleges (54 mapped school and college buildings), concentrated in the centre and the east, so the lanes fill with students in the morning and mid-afternoon.
- Sankhamul Marg and the Bagmati corridor road are the wide through roads; the lanes between them are narrow.
- Sensory detail: school bells, hostel kitchens at dusk, and incense and marigolds at the small lane-corner temples.

Data notes: the default storey count is raised to four because the blocks are visibly taller than the suburb default. Three mapped temples are points inside small buildings; those buildings are styled as brick temples instead of getting a shrine prop. A 23-storey tag on a 58 m² footprint, and 50 m and 40 m height tags on ordinary houses, are replaced by storey counts that fit the footprints.
