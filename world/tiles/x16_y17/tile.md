# x16_y17 — कमलपोखरी (Kamalpokhari)

- Kamalpokhari, "lotus pond", takes its name from the historic Kamal Pokhari pond, a small water body in the north-west of the tile that has shrunk over the years. In the data it is a pond roughly 24 × 18 m at (80, 181), with the National Academy Hall (Rastriya Naachghar) just to the north at (86, 136).
- This is a district of colleges, coaching classes, education consultancies and IT offices. Names such as Model Institute of Technology, Islington College, Rex International Education and Computer Bazar are packed along the roads between Putalisadak and Pashupati Sadak, in blocks that default to four storeys.
- Putalisadak Road runs north-west through the middle of the tile as a primary road; Pashupati Sadak (14 m, four lanes) enters at the east edge near (800, 44) and runs west; Kamaladi Marg, Dillibazar Sadak and Baghbazar Sadak complete the main grid.
- The Russian House (Russian Centre of Science and Culture, set up in 1979) sits on Pashupati Marg at Kamalpokhari, close to a cluster of cinemas around (600–710, 40–60).
- Small Ganesh, Kumari and Kali shrines are scattered across the tile, mostly as map points with no building footprint. Silver City Apartments, tagged at 17 storeys at (764, 638), is the tallest block in the tile.
- Sensory detail: the busy student streets, with college and consultancy signboards on every facade.

Data notes: a duplicate footprint of the Chaarkhaal compound (tagged as a prison in the map data) is removed. Colleges, schools, hospitals, the National Academy Hall and the compound use the institutional style; banks, Kathmandu Plaza and large offices use the glass commercial style; tagged towers of 9 to 17 storeys keep their levels. Roadside shrines are placed at the temple points.
